Six layers. One accountable decision.

Language models interpret requests. Deterministic rules protect the ledger.

01

Message intake

Order-linked text is persisted before inference. Stable IDs deduplicate intake. Ambiguous requests require a customer clarification.

02

Meta intelligence

Muse Spark extracts quantity, price, date, and exact source quotes. Demo mode uses a limited parser and never calls a model.

03

Evidence & policy

Schema checks, exact quote checks, stock arithmetic, and agreed-price preservation. Citation checks do not prove semantic correctness.

04

Resolution planning

Current stock determines feasible allocation, backorders, and catalog substitutions. Delivery capacity remains unverified.

05

Human approval

Customer acceptance is recorded separately and bound to a proposal version. An operator then approves execution after stock revalidation.

06

Durable ledger

A per-user workspace is committed atomically with optimistic concurrency. Every decision leaves an activity event. No external order is modified.

Integration readiness

Live WhatsApp webhooks, voice transcription, ERP synchronization, and real customer messaging are outside this first release.
